description: Walk the whole Doctrina cycle once, guided, on this repository
---

Guide the human through one full Doctrina cycle on **Doctrina**.

1. Run `doctrina onboard`. It marks the steps the tree already shows done
   and prints the current one: what to tell the human, where to ASK, and
   the commands to run.
2. Say the `Tell the human:` sentence in your own words, do the step, and
   stop at every `ASK` until the human answers.
3. Run `doctrina onboard` again and repeat until every step is ticked.

One step per turn. Never skip an `ASK`: the walk exists so the human sees
each decision being made.
